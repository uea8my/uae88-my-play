A fresh accessibility checklist

Small changes that make a big difference.

Updated content • 2026

Accessibility is easier to build into a site when the principles are simple and practical. These points now read with a clearer, more modern voice.

Readable layouts

Use strong contrast, readable sizes, and clear labels so the experience feels comfortable for every visitor.

Keyboard support

Every interactive element should be reachable without a mouse so the experience is more inclusive.

Reduced motion

Respect motion preferences and keep animations from becoming distracting or overwhelming.
