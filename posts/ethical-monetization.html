Ethical monetization for modern game sites

Respect players and you will keep them.

Updated content • 2026

Good monetization feels respectful. It should support the experience rather than interrupt it or make it feel pushy.

Placement

Keep promotional elements away from controls and avoid blocking important actions.

Frequency

Use moderation so the experience does not feel overly repetitive or disruptive.

Transparency

Be upfront about what is being shown and why. Clear information builds trust.
